## E18 Durability

**SIGKILL during live ingest.** A writer process is killed at an arbitrary instant; the log is then replayed into a fresh engine and checked against a reference engine built from exactly the recovered edges.

| corpus | trials | prefix-exact and query-equivalent | edges recovered | replay Meps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bitcoin-temporal | 25 | 25/25 | 131,072-8,585,216 | 6.90 |
| sx-stackoverflow | 25 | 25/25 | 262,144-8,912,896 | 23.44 |

**Injected torn tails and bit flips.** A SIGKILL does not lose page cache, so it cannot produce a torn frame; power loss can. The log is truncated at a random byte, or one byte inside a frame is flipped, and replay must stop at the last intact frame.

## E20 GraphOne, in-category baseline

GraphOne's three timings measure different things. *Batch update* is a memcpy into its circular edge log. *Durable* is that log write()n, with no fsync: the call is left commented out in `src/sgraph.h`, consistent with its own statement that an unplanned shutdown may lose recent data and that recovery depends on an upstream backup such as Kafka. *Make graph* is when archiving into the adjacency has caught up, which is the first point a vertex-centric read sees the edges. Against Gnomon those line up with ingest, the `write_only` row of E18 cost, and ingest plus `finalize()`.
